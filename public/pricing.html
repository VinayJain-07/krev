FAQ

Is there a free trial?

Sign up and explore the demo workspace at no cost. When you're ready to run your own company analyses, choose a plan.

What counts as a token?

Tokens measure the AI work done across analyses, edits, and agent runs. A full company scan uses roughly 180k–300k tokens. The 2M budget supports 6–8 complete scans with room for focused edits.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. When moving to annual, you'll receive credit for any remaining monthly balance.

What AI providers are supported?

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and OpenRouter. Bring your own key — your credentials are encrypted at rest.
