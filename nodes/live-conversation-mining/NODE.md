# Live Conversation Mining

## Purpose

Find current, relevant conversations that can inform content or human-reviewed responses while preserving context, recency, and platform boundaries.

## Process

1. Define the target customer, job or pain, topic vocabulary, geography, and acceptable recency window.
2. Search timestamped public pages or approved authenticated platform data. Store the source URL, platform, author or organization when public, published date, and access date.
3. Verify that the destination conversation is real, relevant, open to participation, and not already resolved or inappropriate for outreach.
4. Summarize the exact question or language before proposing a response. Distinguish discovered text from interpretation.
5. Recommend helpful, transparent, non-promotional Reddit answers. For LinkedIn and X, add an original insight and do not copy another author's wording.
6. Mark public search discovery as limited coverage; never describe it as complete account history or authenticated platform analytics.

## Output standard

Return conversation, source evidence, recency, audience fit, response angle, draft or next action, risk, and human-review requirement. Do not recommend automated posting without an approved connector and explicit review.
