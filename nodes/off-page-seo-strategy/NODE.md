# Off-Page SEO Strategy

Create a risk-controlled off-page growth system. Optimize for topical relevance, editorial value, qualified referral demand, brand authority, and measurable business outcomes. Do not optimize for raw backlink volume.

## Evidence rules

- Treat company documents, current site evidence, backlink data, competitor evidence, and user-provided research as inputs with different confidence levels.
- Treat the approved master workflow as a candidate taxonomy only. Its authority scores, follow attributes, pricing, traffic claims, and automation claims are not verified facts.
- Do not invent prospects, contact details, traffic, rankings, authority metrics, link attributes, response rates, or commercial results.
- Label assumptions and missing inputs. Keep third-party authority metrics comparative and source-dated when they are available.
- Include a target URL only when it is in the master-derived allowlist and passes endpoint validation at export time. Omit failed, parked, unrelated, or obsolete destinations.

## Strategy workflow

1. Connect business goal to SEO goal, priority topic, target page, off-page mechanism, and KPI.
2. Diagnose authority baseline, competitor link gaps, unlinked mentions, citation consistency, community presence, and linkable-asset readiness.
3. Select only opportunity types that are relevant, editorially defensible, commercially useful, and realistically achievable.
4. Score each prospect from 0 to 10 using the model in [references/workbook-contract.md](references/workbook-contract.md).
5. Build a 30/60/90-day roadmap with an owner, due date, dependency, KPI, risk control, and validation step for every action.
6. Separate safe automation from decisions requiring human review. Final prospect qualification, factual claims, personalization, relationship judgment, and placement approval always require a person.

## Output contract

- The primary and only generated artifact is XLSX. Do not generate PDF or PPTX for this report.
- Follow the sheet structure, formulas, validation lists, link controls, and quality gate in [references/workbook-contract.md](references/workbook-contract.md).
- Make the workbook useful before any manual formatting: frozen headers, filters, readable widths, concise instructions, formula-driven summaries, restrained color, and no decorative dashboard clutter.
- Every active prospect row must contain a validated official HTTPS URL from the master workflow registry and a validation timestamp/status.
- Keep rejected candidates out of the active prospect and outreach sheets. A validation log may name rejected candidates and reasons, but must not expose invalid hyperlinks.
- Every recommended activity needs a reason, owner, KPI, risk, priority, and next action.

## Quality gate

Before returning the workbook, confirm that it contains no invented metrics or contacts, no unvalidated active URL, no duplicate prospect rows, no formula errors, no empty required headers, and no unsupported link-quality claim.
