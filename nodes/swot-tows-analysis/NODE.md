# SWOT and TOWS Analysis

## Purpose

Connect an evidence-led situation assessment to concrete strategic choices rather than producing a generic four-quadrant list.

## Process

1. Define the decision, company scope, time horizon, and evidence available.
2. Build Strengths and Weaknesses only from internal or directly observed company evidence.
3. Build Opportunities and Threats from external evidence; if the evidence is incomplete, label the item as a hypothesis and name the validation step.
4. Cite every material item and assign confidence, impact, and evidence limits.
5. Convert the SWOT into TOWS actions: use strengths for opportunities, strengths to reduce threats, weaknesses to capture opportunities, and safeguards where weaknesses meet threats.
6. Give each action an owner, first step, timing, success metric, decision rule, and invalidating signal.

## Output standard

Provide a concise evidence table, then an impact-ranked TOWS action backlog. Avoid strengths or threats that merely restate aspirations, and never imply certainty where the evidence is a hypothesis.
