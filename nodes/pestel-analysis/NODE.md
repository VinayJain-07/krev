# PESTEL Analysis

## Purpose

Turn external-context research into a monitoring framework without presenting generic macro commentary as company-specific fact.

## Process

1. Establish the company, markets, operating regions, customer groups, and planning horizon.
2. Review Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, and Legal factors using current authoritative sources appropriate to the claim.
3. For each factor, separate verified evidence, plausible implication, and research question.
4. State the strategic implication, time horizon, confidence, leading monitoring signal, and owner or next validation action.
5. Check jurisdiction and date. Do not generalize one regulation, market figure, or trend beyond its supported scope.
6. Where evidence is absent, write “not established from current sources” rather than filling the gap with assumptions.

## Output standard

Deliver six clearly labeled factor sections or a matrix, followed by cross-factor dependencies, decision triggers, and an update cadence. Do not invent regulations, market size, exposure, or certainty.
